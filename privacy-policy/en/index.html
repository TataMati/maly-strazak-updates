This policy applies to the Android game “Mały Strażak” (“Little Firefighter”). External services may differ between test builds and the version distributed through Google Play.

1. Who is the app for?

“Mały Strażak” is a game intended for an audience that includes children. Basic gameplay does not require an account or the submission of a name, email address, or phone number. The game does not require access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location for gameplay.

2. Information stored on the device

Game preferences, such as sound and language settings, and any gameplay progress supported by a given version may be stored locally on the device. We do not operate a separate player account or profile database to which this information is uploaded. Local information can be cleared through Android app settings or by uninstalling the app; any device backups depend on the device's own settings.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Some versions of the app, including test builds, may display banner ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Not every build contains an active advertising module. When this module is used, Google may automatically receive data such as an IP address (which may reveal approximate location), device or app identifiers, app launches and ad interactions, and diagnostics and performance information. This information may be used to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and diagnose technical issues.

The game is also intended for children. The planned ad-enabled release uses child-directed treatment, an under-age-of-consent tag, a maximum ad content rating of G, and removes Android's advertising ID permission. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether ads may be requested. Children do not receive personalized advertising. Removing advertising ID permission does not disable all technical identifiers. We verify the store release and ad suppliers before publishing.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps.

4. Network connections and updates

Depending on the version, the app may check for updates through Google Play or download approved game assets over HTTPS. A service provider handling such a request may receive an IP address and standard connection metadata, such as a request timestamp and technical information about the device. We do not use these connections to build profiles of children.

5. Purposes and recipients

Technical data processing may be necessary to operate network functions, provide updates, and maintain security; when advertising is enabled, it may also support ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Depending on enabled features, recipients may include Google (Google Play and AdMob) and the hosting provider for game updates. We do not independently sell players' personal information. Where required by law, appropriate consent mechanisms apply.

6. Retention

Locally stored settings and progress remain on the device until deleted or the app is uninstalled, subject to any device backup arrangements. We do not keep separate player accounts on our own servers. Google and other service providers determine retention periods for data they process under their own policies and service settings; not all of those periods are under our control.

7. Security

Game asset downloads use HTTPS. External service providers are responsible for protecting data within their services. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's and parents' rights

Parents and guardians may contact us about a child's privacy and, where applicable under law, request access to, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data processing. Contact bravorygames@gmail.com. Because we do not create individual player accounts, we may not hold enough information to identify a specific player. Locally stored data can be removed using Android settings. Requests involving data independently managed by Google may need to be addressed to Google.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when game features, software libraries, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain accessible at the same web address.
