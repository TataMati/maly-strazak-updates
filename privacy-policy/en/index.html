1. Scope

This policy describes privacy practices for the Android mobile game “Little Firefighter” (“Mały Strażak”). For questions about data and privacy, contact the publisher at the email address above.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The app is being prepared to display advertising through Google AdMob. Depending on configuration and ad availability, the advertising service may process technical information, including device information, diagnostic data, IP address and interactions with advertisements, in accordance with its own privacy terms. The availability of advertisements is not guaranteed.

See Google Privacy Policy and AdMob policies for information about Google's practices.

3. Children and families

The game is also intended for younger audiences. In-app advertising should be configured to comply with Google Play's Families policies, including restrictions on ad personalization and children's advertising identifiers. For questions about ad behavior, contact the publisher.

4. Information submitted voluntarily

If a user or guardian contacts us by email, we receive the contents of their message and use it to respond to the inquiry. Please do not send children's personal information.

5. Security and user rights

Questions and requests to access, correct or delete information can be sent to bravorygames@gmail.com. Information processed directly by Google is also subject to Google's own policies and controls.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or third-party services change. The current version will be available at this address.
